Get to Know Me

A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Audrey! I'm an Italian content creator living in South Korea, with a passion for fitness, beauty, wellness, and travel.

Having traveled to over 50 countries, I've learned that some of the most beautiful experiences come from stepping outside your comfort zone, embracing new cultures, and being open to the unexpected.

Through my content, I share little pieces of my everyday life, my fitness and self-care routines, and the adventures that make life exciting.

My goal is to inspire women to feel confident, take care of themselves, explore more, and create a life they genuinely love.

— Audrey
